Redlands Kindness Project

Recognising the people who quietly make Redland City a better place.

About the Project

The Redlands Kindness Project is a Lions Club of Redland City initiative that shines a light on community members who quietly make a positive difference — neighbours, volunteers, and everyday local heroes. Anyone in the community can nominate someone for recognition, and selected nominees are acknowledged through our club communications and invited to an end-of-year celebration.

Before we publicly share any nominee's story, name, or photo, we'll contact you and the nominee to get their permission first.

How It Works

1. Nominate

Fill in the form below with the nominee's name and a short note about why they deserve recognition.

2. Review

Our project team reviews nominations and follows up with nominators to confirm details.

3. Celebrate

Selected nominees are recognised through our communications and invited to a club celebration event.

Key Dates

  • September 2026: Community nominations open.
  • October 2026 – May 2027: Nominees recognised on a rolling basis.
  • June 2027: Recipients celebrated at a club event.
