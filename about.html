About Our Club

Local service, global reach — part of the world's largest volunteer service organization.

Our Story

The Lions Club of Redland City is a chartered member of Lions Clubs International, a network of over 1.4 million volunteer members in more than 200 countries working to serve their communities. Locally, our club brings together residents of Redland City who share a commitment to hands-on service, fellowship, and giving back.

We organise and support projects that make a genuine difference — from fundraising for local families and sight and hearing programs, to environmental clean-ups, youth support, and disaster relief.

Our Mission

To empower volunteers to serve their communities, meet humanitarian needs, encourage peace, and promote international understanding through Lions clubs.

What We Stand For

Service

We roll up our sleeves for practical, local projects that directly help people in Redland City.

Integrity

Every dollar raised is directed to genuine community and humanitarian causes.

Fellowship

We build lasting friendships while working together toward a shared purpose.

Our Meetings

We meet regularly to plan projects, catch up, and welcome new and prospective members. Contact us for our current meeting schedule and location.
