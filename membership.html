Become a Member

Meet great people, build real skills, and make a genuine difference in Redland City.

Why Join the Lions?

Make an Impact

Take part in hands-on projects that directly benefit people in your own community.

Build Connections

Meet like-minded locals and build friendships that last well beyond club meetings.

Develop Skills

Grow leadership, organisational, and event-planning experience in a supportive club environment.

Join a Global Family

Become part of Lions Clubs International, connecting you to over 1.4 million members worldwide.

How to Join

Getting started is easy — come along to one of our meetings as a guest, meet the members, and learn more about our current projects. No experience or special skills required, just a willingness to help.
